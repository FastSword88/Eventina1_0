# 🎬 Movie Night Scheduler Bot

A sleek Discord bot designed to streamline and automate movie night planning with your community.

## ✨ Features
- **Automated Scheduling** – Easily plan and coordinate movie nights
- **Reminder System** – Automated notifications for upcoming events
- **Calendar Sync** – Keep track of scheduled movie nights
- **Customizable Settings** – Tailor the bot to your server's needs

Perfect for gaming communities, friend groups, and anyone looking to bring people together through shared movie experiences.
